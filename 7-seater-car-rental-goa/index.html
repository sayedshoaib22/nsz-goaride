Frequently asked questions

Which seven-seater cars are listed in Goa?

The current NSZ Goa Ride fleet listings identify the Maruti Ertiga, Toyota Innova Crysta and Toyota Innova Hycross as seven-seat options. Confirm date-specific availability with the team.

Are automatic seven-seater cars available?

The fleet listing shows automatic options for the Maruti Ertiga and Toyota Innova Crysta, and an automatic Toyota Innova Hycross. Confirm which vehicle is available for your dates before booking.

How much does a seven-seater rental cost?

The published listing shows the Ertiga at ₹2,200 per day manual or ₹3,000 automatic; the Innova Crysta at ₹3,000 manual or ₹3,500 automatic; and the Innova Hycross automatic at ₹3,500 per day. Confirm the final price and terms for your dates.

Is a security deposit required?

Confirm the security deposit amount and refund terms for your selected vehicle and dates with the team before booking.

Can I arrange pickup at an airport or railway station?

Ask the team to confirm pickup availability at Dabolim Airport, Mopa Airport or Madgaon Railway Station for your dates, and whether any pickup charge applies.

How do I check vehicle availability?

Use the booking form or contact NSZ Goa Ride on WhatsApp with your dates, preferred vehicle and transmission, passenger count and pickup point.
