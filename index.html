04 — Quick Answers

Frequently asked
questions

Everything you need to know about booking a car rental in Goa with NSZ Goa Ride.

What is the cheapest car rental option near Dabolim Airport?

Our Maruti Ignis and Swift models are the most affordable options starting at ₹1,200 per day. Both are fuel-efficient, compact, and perfect for solo travelers or couples exploring Goa.

How do I book a car rental in Goa with NSZ Goa Ride?

Book through our website or directly via WhatsApp at +91 8262812997. Simply send us your requirements (dates, vehicle type, pickup location), and we'll confirm availability and send a quote within minutes.

Are there automatic transmission cars available?

Yes, we have a range of automatic vehicles including Maruti Swift Dzire Automatic, Hyundai Creta Automatic, and premium automatic luxury sedans. Specify your preference when booking.

Can you deliver a car to my hotel or apartment?

Absolutely! We provide doorstep delivery across North Goa, South Goa, and to major hotels in Panaji, Colva, and Candolim. Just provide your exact location when booking.

What documents are required for car rental?

You'll need a valid driving license (Indian or International), identity proof (Passport, Aadhar, or Voter ID), and a security deposit. All documents are returned after the rental period.

Is fuel included in the rental price?

No, fuel is charged separately at current market rates. You can prepay for fuel or refuel the vehicle yourself before returning it. We provide full tank at pickup.

What is your cancellation policy?

We offer flexible cancellation up to 24 hours before pickup with full refund. Cancellations within 24 hours may incur a charge. Contact us for specific details on your booking.

Are children car seats available?

Yes, we provide child safety seats (infant, booster) on request at a nominal charge. Specify your child's age when booking to ensure the correct seat is available.

What happens if the car breaks down?

We provide 24/7 roadside assistance and vehicle replacement. In case of a breakdown, call us immediately at +91 8262812997, and we'll arrange a replacement vehicle within 30 minutes.

Can I drive the rental car outside Goa?

Yes, but with restrictions. Outstation travel is permitted with prior approval and additional charges. Routes like Goa to Karnataka borders or beyond require special permission. Contact us for details.
