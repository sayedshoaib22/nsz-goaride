Cancellation and Refund Policy

We understand that travel plans change. This policy explains how cancellations and refundable deposits are handled for confirmed rentals.

Cancellation timeline

  • More than 7 days before pickup: Full security-deposit refund, less a ₹500 cancellation fee.
  • 4 to 7 days before pickup: 50% of the security deposit is refundable.
  • 1 to 3 days before pickup: 25% of the security deposit is refundable.
  • Less than 24 hours or no-show: The security deposit is non-refundable.

Refund process

  1. Request cancellation by WhatsApp, phone, or email.
  2. Wait for written confirmation from NSZ Goa Ride.
  3. Approved refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days.
  4. Refunds are returned to the original payment method where possible.

Important notes

  • Rental charges, fuel charges, and additional service fees are non-refundable after confirmation.
  • Damage, traffic, or late-return charges are separate from the security deposit refund.
  • Exceptional circumstances may be reviewed case by case.
